A working model of Vikram's leaderboard spec (draft v1, project Rabbit Hole) — pick a game, a cycle, and a day of the week below and watch the rules play out.
Dealers, scores and attempts here are all invented for exploring the rules — regenerated fresh each time this tab loads, not real dealer data.

Identity & games

One global display name per dealer — username or real name — used across every game.

3 games at launch, each with its own independent leaderboard. No combined cross-game score.

Scoring & retries

Only a win posts a score. Up to 3 attempts/game/day, each a fresh puzzle. Best winning score of the day counts.

Cycles

Daily resets each active day. Weekly aggregates Mon–Sat. Sunday is free play — nothing posts. Weekly finalizes Saturday, stays visible through Sunday, resets Monday.

Ranking & visibility

Tied scores share a rank (skip the next number). Public view shows top 20; every dealer can see their own rank privately.
